For radios with horizontal LCD 480x272 like Radiomaster TX16S or FrSky X12
- rename splash_480x272.png to splash.png and move to /IMAGES folder on your SD card.

For radios with horizontal LCD 480x320 like Jumper T15
- rename splash_480x320.png to splash.png and move to /IMAGES folder on your SD card.

For radios with vertical LCD 320x480 like FlySky NV14 or EL18
- rename splash_320x480.png to splash.png and move to /IMAGES folder on your SD card.
