## Installation

It's easy!

- Put this `Stork_TENS` folder into your EdgeTX SDCard at `THEMES/`. You have seen this sample SD Card we got here, now go place it as this one does
- On Main screen, press `SYS` top left
- `PAGE >` repeatedly until you go to `🎨 Themes selection` tab. Or click `🎨 Themes selection` tab.
- Scroll down or Jogwheel down and choose `Stork_TENS` theme.
- `ENTER` (Jogwheel press) & `Set Active`.
- Enjoy!

### `⚡🫀🩺 TENS`?

Desktop Pacemaker. Temporarily attached pacemaker system. **Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation** that is for chest, heart organ.

We are cardiophilia. We chose this name as an inspiration to this cool device.

Cardio-TENS is a Medical equipment to debug heart organ. Usually used to hold heart rate to debug something wrong with heart like arrythmia or something. Often is an included feature with Defib machines as a mode in its dial.
