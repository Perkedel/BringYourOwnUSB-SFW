## Haard to fly

use full sized Gimbal RC! avoid small ones, coz it's no different than your gamepad.

We recommend (to name few known ones)

- Radiomaster
  - Boxer
  - TX15
  - TX16s series
  - MT12 Pistol
  - any remote both flagship & DIY that has this Radiomaster's AG series Full Size gimbals. particularly 01 & 02 ones.
- Jumper
  - ...
- Flysky
  - ...
- Van Eletronische, RemoteMod
  - TENS-01 Dual Joystick
  - TENS-11 / Selonjoran Pistol Radio
  - Murah Series *for RemoteMod* Aeromodeling Color/BW.
    - avoid the one that has `Menciut` (small Gimbal)'
  - Murah Series *for RemoteMod* Car Color/BW.

And remember, the Remotes above supports USB HID connection. Meaning, you can use it as a gamepad, but way bigger joystick, wider controls, hence better performance.
